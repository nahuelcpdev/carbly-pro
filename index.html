How it works

Three steps. No database trawl.

Carbly never asks you to type a portion size in grams, scroll a 40,000-row food list, or pick the wrong Caesar salad. Just point and shoot.

  1. 01 Snap

    Point your camera at the plate. Hit the shutter. The frame routes to the AI, which only processes the request — never stores it, never trains on it.

    ≈ 0.2s
  2. 02 Verify

    Review the AI estimate. Adjust portion or add a voice note if the plate's bigger than it looks. The estimate updates live.

    ≈ 4s
  3. 03 Log

    Tap save. Your net carbs, macros, and electrolyte totals update across Dashboard, Food Log, and Carbly Score instantly.

    ≈ 1 tap

Privacy contract Photos are analyzed in memory and discarded after the result returns. AI providers process the request only — zero retention, zero training, no human review.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Short, factual. If you have a question that isn't here, email us at the address in the footer.

How accurate is the AI estimate?
Carbly estimates fall within ±12% of label values on typical plates. Adding a voice note ("6 oz chicken thigh, ranch on the side") tightens the range. Every result carries an "AI estimate" disclaimer — verify against nutrition labels when they're available.
What does the free tier include?
3 AI scans/day, unlimited barcode scans, fasting timer, electrolyte tracking, streak with weekly grace day, and the full net-carb dashboard. No card required.
Do you store my photos?
No. Photos are routed to the AI provider, analyzed in memory, and discarded. Only the structured result text (e.g. "4g net carbs · 32g fat") is saved to your account.
Is Carbly on Android?
iOS first, iOS only — for now. Android is on the roadmap; the cross-platform groundwork is underway. Sign up below to be notified when it ships.
Is Carbly medical advice?
No. Carbly is a tracker. It reports factual numbers — calories, macros, electrolytes — and never tells you what to eat, when to fast, or what your "ketosis state" is. Talk to a healthcare provider for dietary decisions.
